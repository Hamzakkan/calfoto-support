CalFoto Support

Help with scanning, calendar events, and general questions.

How CalFoto works

CalFoto reads a photo, screenshot, or typed description of an event and turns it into a calendar entry. Tap Scan to take a photo or choose one from your library, or tap Type an Event to describe it in a sentence. Review the details on the confirmation screen, then add it to your calendar.

Common questions

Why does the app need photo library access?
Only to let you pick a screenshot or saved photo to scan. CalFoto only reads the specific photo you select — it never scans or uploads the rest of your library.

Why does the app need calendar access?
To check your existing schedule for conflicts before adding a new event, and to actually add the event once you approve it. CalFoto never adds anything without your confirmation.

The AI misread my flyer or message — what can I do?
You can edit every field (title, date, time, location, notes, reminders) on the review screen before saving. If a photo is blurry or hard to read, try retaking it with better lighting, or use "Type an Event" instead.

I've hit my monthly scan limit — what happens?
Free scans reset on the 1st of each month. You'll see a clear message if you've reached the limit for the current month.

Does CalFoto work without an internet connection?
Scanning and typing require an internet connection, since event details are processed by AI. Viewing your History and Upcoming events works offline, since that data is stored on your device.

How do I delete a scan from my history?
Open the History tab, and use the delete option on any entry — you can choose to remove it from CalFoto's history only, or also delete it from your calendar.
